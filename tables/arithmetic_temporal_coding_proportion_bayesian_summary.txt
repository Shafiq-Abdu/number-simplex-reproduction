
========================================================================
BAYESIAN ANALYSIS: TEMPORAL NUMBER-CODING PROPORTION
========================================================================

SCOPE
-----
This analysis concerns the proportion of neurons classified as
number-coding using the paper-style raw permutation criterion:

    raw p < 0.05

It does NOT estimate the proportion of neurons that are biologically
"truly number-coding".

It also does NOT replace the separate multiple-comparison / FDR analysis.


========================================================================
OBSERVED DATA
========================================================================

Valid neuron-level permutation tests:
544

Neurons classified as coding using raw p < 0.05:
160

Observed pooled coding proportion:
29.412%


========================================================================
SIMPLE BAYESIAN BETA-BINOMIAL MODEL
========================================================================

Model:

    theta ~ Beta(alpha, beta)

    K | theta ~ Binomial(N, theta)

where theta represents the probability that a valid neuron is
classified as number-coding under the raw p < 0.05 procedure.

Prior:

    theta ~ Beta(1, 1)

Since Beta(1,1) is uniform, this prior does not favor any particular
coding probability between 0 and 1.

Posterior:

    theta | data ~ Beta(161, 385)

POSTERIOR RESULTS
========================================================================

Posterior mean:
29.487%

Posterior median:
29.462%

95% Bayesian credible interval:
[25.739%, 33.378%]


Posterior probabilities:

    = 99.999993%

    = 99.330784%

P(theta > 25% | data)

P(theta > 30% | data)

P(25% < theta < 35% | data)
    = 98.795649%


========================================================================
IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
========================================================================

The posterior is concentrated around approximately 29.5%.

However, this model treats the 544 neuron-level classifications as
exchangeable Binomial observations.

Therefore, this posterior describes uncertainty in the RAW
CLASSIFICATION RATE produced by the p < 0.05 procedure.

It should NOT be interpreted as:

    "Approximately 29.5% of neurons truly encode number."

A neuron with p = 0.0004 and a neuron with p = 0.049 are both treated
identically as coding = 1, while a neuron with p = 0.051 is treated as
coding = 0.

Thus, thresholding discards information about the strength of
neuron-level evidence.

========================================================================
HETEROGENEITY INTERPRETATION
========================================================================

The observed subject-to-subject SD was:

    4.379 pp

whereas the approximate SD expected from Binomial sampling alone was:

    6.663 pp

Therefore, the observed subject variation was NOT greater than the
variation expected under the simple Binomial model.

The estimated extra variance was negative before truncation:

    -0.00252275

Since a variance cannot be negative, the additional between-subject
variance estimate is effectively at the boundary:

    extra between-subject variance ≈ 0

This explains why the empirical-Bayes Beta-Binomial optimization
returned extremely large alpha and beta values.

This should NOT be interpreted as proof that all subjects have exactly
the same biological coding probability.

With only 11 subjects, the present data simply do not provide evidence
for additional between-subject heterogeneity beyond the variation
expected under this simple classification model.


========================================================================
RELATION TO THE FDR RESULT
========================================================================

The Bayesian Beta-Binomial analysis and the FDR analysis answer
different questions.

Bayesian raw-proportion analysis:

    "How uncertain are we about the rate of raw p < 0.05
     classifications?"

FDR analysis:

    "Which individual neuron-level discoveries remain sufficiently
     strong after accounting for the large family of statistical tests?"

Therefore, a stable raw coding proportion near 29% can coexist with
zero neurons surviving BH-FDR correction.

These results are not contradictory.


========================================================================
CURRENT CONCLUSION
========================================================================

Under the paper-style raw p < 0.05 criterion, approximately 29% of the
valid neurons in the reproduction are classified as number-coding.

A simple Bayesian Beta-Binomial model places the raw classification
rate primarily in the range:

    25.74% to 33.38%

However, this analysis does not establish that the same percentage of
neurons truly encode number.

The hierarchical Beta-Binomial extension did not reveal meaningful
additional between-subject heterogeneity and effectively collapsed
toward the pooled model.

Further Bayesian modeling of the biological "true coding" proportion
would require modeling richer neuron-level evidence rather than only
the binary raw p < 0.05 labels.
